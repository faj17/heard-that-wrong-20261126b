# Sorry, I Heard That Wrong

A one-line apology from a smart speaker that misheard "wake me at seven" as "wake me in heaven" — in 113 characters, for make.fun's "Sorry, I Heard That Wrong" bounty. `main.py` writes the apology to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Sorry, I heard 'wake me at seven' as 'wake me in heaven,' so I have booked you a one-way ticket and packed light.

The speaker heard a wake-up call as a final destination, and — ever helpful — already booked the trip and packed light. No return ticket was mentioned.
